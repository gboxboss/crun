## OMISSIONS
- Newer free, commercial-OK 10 m imagery exists. ESA WorldCover S2 RGBNIR annual composites for 2020 and 2021 are CC BY 4.0 on AWS (s3://esa-worldcover-s2), and the Sentinel-2 L2A 120 m mosaic is CC BY 4.0. Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem Sentinel-2 mosaics, released under the free Copernicus licence, are another route; the licence terms need checking. This removes much of the dependence on the 2016 EOX mosaic and on a paid EOX licence.
- geoBoundaries is about 38% share-alike (225/715 ODbL, 45 CC BY-SA). The Territory Composer's 'include_units' and the proprietary flywheel library must exclude ODbL and BY-SA units, or the curated database becomes an ODbL derivative. Overture Divisions is also ODbL. Build units only from Natural Earth admin-1 (public domain) or per-file CC BY/PD geoBoundaries.
- The Nominatim policy now explicitly bans embedding the public API in no-code, low-code or vibe-coding platforms and reselling geocoding results. An AI video generator fits that description, so even dev or demo use of the public API is risky.
- Mapbox Product Terms (2026-02-05) section 1.9 bans bulk or automated queries. An unattended render farm breaches it even with video rights. Section 1.7.4 does sell 'Purchased Video Rights', so Mapbox is a paid option, not a closed door. Mapbox GL JS v2+ is proprietary; use MapLibre (BSD-3).
- The Copernicus DEM licence requires a liability-disclaimer sentence in the legal notice, which the report's credit block omits. GEBCO_2026 appears to be the current grid (2025 is superseded); update the citation and DOI.
- Cliopatria provenance can be traced from the repository. original_map_images/ holds 507 PNG maps made by Andrew Tollefson in 2014, which formed the basis of the dataset. Legal review should ask Seshat or Tollefson which atlases were traced and whether Tollefson licensed his work for CC BY release. The polygons have been 'provided for reference' and revised by historians since.
- The Natural Earth POV variants are only at 1:10m. Natural Earth warns that democracies may be shown de facto, so the IND POV is not a legal safe harbour under India's 1961 Act. s.2(3) means only the Government can bring a complaint. From prior knowledge, not re-checked here: Indian police have filed criminal cases against platforms over incorrect maps (e.g. Twitter India, 2021), so the risk is real for a service with Indian users. Commission or buy a Survey of India-conformant outline for an India preset.
- Renderer licensing affects per-video cost and is not covered. Remotion, a common React video renderer whose official maps skill targets exactly this kind of video, is source-available, not open source. It is free only for individuals and companies of up to 3 employees; larger companies need a paid Company License, and automated rendering is priced per render (remotion.pro). MapLibre, deck.gl and three.js are permissive.
- Hot-linking risks are not called out. The OSM tile usage policy forbids bulk or prefetch use of tile.openstreetmap.org. Protomaps says hotlinking its builds is discouraged and URLs may change. EOX's WMTS service terms may differ from the data license. Download or buy everything and self-host; never render from community tile servers.
- A 2017 EOX cloudless edition exists (OSM editor layer index) and the report doesn't mention it; ask EOX for its license. Overture Places is CDLA-Permissive-2.0 or Apache-2.0 depending on source, so record the license per feature.
- Tilezen/AWS Terrain Tiles also require an ArcticDEM (DigitalGlobe/NSF) credit, which the report's list omits. That is another reason to prefer Copernicus DEM or Mapterhorn with a single credit list.

## RELIABILITY
Mostly reliable on open-data licensing. Checks against primary sources hosted on GitHub confirmed: Protomaps, OpenFreeMap (except one point), OpenMapTiles, Natural Earth, Cliopatria (row and polity counts reproduced exactly), aourednik, Nominatim, Photon, Cloudflare R2 pricing, Chronas, Pleiades, Iconify and milsymbol. Commercial-provider terms (Mapbox, Google, Esri, EOX, GEBCO, GADM, CShapes) could only be matched against third-party quotes because the primary sites are blocked here; they are consistent with the report. MapTiler, Euratlas and Google Earth Studio remain unverified. Decision-relevant errors: the claim that EOX 2016 is the only free commercial 10 m mosaic is wrong, because ESA WorldCover's 2020/2021 composites are CC BY 4.0. geoBoundaries' share-alike exposure is understated at about 38%, which undermines the planned proprietary Territory Composer. Copernicus DEM obligations are incomplete. The Mapbox analysis misses the automated-query ban and the purchasable video rights. The stack recommendation (self-hosted open data, MapLibre, Natural Earth plus Protomaps, Cliopatria behind a curated layer) is still sound, and the per-video data-cost estimate is credible.
